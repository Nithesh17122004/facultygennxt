How listing works

  1. Register your institution. Provide your official email, website, address and a signed authorisation letter. Our team verifies the institution before any vacancy can be published.
  2. Create the vacancy. Add the designation, department, eligibility, pay details, deadline and the official notification. Drafts are private; submissions enter the review queue.
  3. Pass the checklist. We confirm the institution, recruiter authority, advertisement authenticity, application route, deadline and eligibility details.
  4. Publish and manage. Once approved, the vacancy goes live with structured data, search visibility and an application workflow you control.

What we check before publication

  • Institution identity, registration and current verification status
  • Recruiter authority to publish for the institution
  • Vacancy details against the official advertisement or notification
  • Official application URL, email or internal application route
  • Application deadline and eligibility conditions

Listings that cannot be verified are returned with a reason and can be corrected and resubmitted.
