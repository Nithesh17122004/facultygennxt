Why FacultyNxtGen

A recruitment platform built on checks, not claims.

Academic hiring depends on precise eligibility, authentic notices and deadlines that mean something. We verify those details before a vacancy appears publicly.

How verification works

Verified institutions

Registration certificates and authorisation letters are reviewed by our team before an institution can publish.

Official notifications

Each vacancy is checked against the recruitment notice, including eligibility, pay and application method.

Private documents

CVs and certificates use signed, time-limited access. Recruiters see only what you submit to their vacancy.

Clear lifecycle

Applications move through a visible status workflow, with candidate notifications at every decision.
