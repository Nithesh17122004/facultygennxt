Security practices

  • Use a unique password of at least 10 characters.
  • Enable an authenticator app rather than SMS where possible.
  • Never share OTPs or verification links with anyone, including someone claiming to be a recruiter.
  • Report suspicious emails or vacancy links to support.

Session records

Sign-in, password and MFA events are recorded in security audit logs with IP address and timestamp for fraud investigation. These logs are visible only to authorised administrators.
